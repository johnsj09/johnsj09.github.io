    My story

    Let's go back in time.

    2025 — Present

    01/ 05

    1. Dec 2025 — Present

      Independent Researcher & Freelance LIMS Developer

      Nottingham · freelance with HTIC, IIT Madras

      • Independent research on memory and sleep, including the hippocampal-model (C-HORSE) manuscript in preparation with Dr Nick Myers.
      • Since Sept 2026: freelance LIMS developer with HTIC, IIT Madras.
      • Building independent LIMS: sample management, workflows and data visualisation, plus instrument integration and lab-data work for small teams, alongside part-time work in a lab.

    2. 2024 — 2025

      MSc Computational Neuroscience, Cognition & AI

      University of Nottingham · Distinction

      • Dissertation with Dr Nick Myers: a hippocampal model (C-HORSE) of how rule learning and episodic memory share one circuit.
      • Built a self-driving PiCar on a Raspberry Pi 4: CNN steering and speed, plus an SSD-MobileNetV2 detector at 0.96 mAP.

    3. 2022 — 2024

      Lab Informatics Engineer

      Zifo RnD Solutions · with GSK and BMS

      • Designed LIMS data models, workflows and metadata structures for regulated pharma labs.
      • Wrote Java integrations to AWS S3, digital pathology and lab instruments, halving data-processing time, in GxP-validated releases.

    4. 2019 — 2022

      Research Assistant, Vascular Biology

      SASTRA University, India

      • Alongside my BTech: designed and ran ischaemia-reperfusion experiments; worked hands-on with SEM and TEM on tissue.
      • Co-first author, Naunyn-Schmiedeberg’s Archives of Pharmacology (2023).

    5. 2018 — 2022

      BTech Biotechnology

      SASTRA Deemed University, India

      • Where the lab bench and the code editor first met: biology, electronics and a lot of data.
      • The start of an interest in how brains, and the instruments that study them, keep their records.
