How does a memory system learn a general rule and still remember the one case that breaks it? I tested this in C-HORSE, a model of the hippocampus, using a task I designed: a museum whose rules change with the season.

Neural dynamics

Two ion channels are enough to make a cell oscillate

The Morris-Lecar model of the barnacle giant muscle fibre, which I analysed with numerical solvers and phase-plane methods. Raise the current and the cell starts to fire. Raise it further and the rhythm stops.

Decision-making / group project

Do cognitive models explain behaviour, or only predict it?

Five model classes fitted to mouse reversal-learning data: logistic regression, Q-learning, Bayesian inference, switching GRUs and disentangled RNNs. A switching GRU captured action-switching that the baseline models missed, and stayed interpretable.

Auditory cortex / group project

How the cortex responds to sound in bursts

Replicated the Loebel et al. (2007) model of primary auditory cortex, where recurrent networks with short-term synaptic depression produce population spikes. We extended it to test background noise and signal-to-noise ratio.
