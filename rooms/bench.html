The Bench

Before the code, there was the bench.

I started in a wet lab, studying how heart tissue survives losing and regaining its blood supply. That time is why I build lab software the way scientists need it.

Ischaemia and reperfusion, in one line

Cut the blood supply and heart tissue stops working; restore it and the return itself does damage. We studied whether a drug could soften that second blow. Toggle it on.

The work

  1. Ran the experimentsDesigned and carried out ischaemia-reperfusion studies as a research assistant in vascular biology.
  2. Looked closelyHands-on scanning and transmission electron microscopy (SEM and TEM) on tissue.
  3. PublishedCo-first author on a peer-reviewed paper on sodium thiosulfate and ischaemia-reperfusion injury.
  4. Learned the lab from the insideWhich is why, later, I could build software that scientists actually use.
